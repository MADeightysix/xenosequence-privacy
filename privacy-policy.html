Privacy Policy — XenoSequence: Evolution Effective date: October 25, 2026 Developer: MADeightysix Contact: mitchelldepriest+appsupport@gmail.com About the app XenoSequence: Evolution is a Python learning game intended for ages 13 and up. This policy covers the Android app provided by MADeightysix. Learning and game data The app stores lesson code, drafts, test results, lesson progress, save timestamps, creature settings, local game scores, earned points, and cosmetic selections on your device. Python exercises run locally in a restricted teaching environment. The app does not send your lesson code, pet name, scores, or progress to a developer-operated server. Your images If you choose an image through Android's file picker, the app reads that selected image and makes a local copy for your chosen background, hat, or accessory. The app does not upload these images to the developer or provide public image sharing. Only choose images you have permission to use. Do not put sensitive personal information in lesson code or custom images. Purchases The full game is offered through a one-time Google Play purchase. Google handles payment information and payment processing under its own terms and privacy policy. The app receives purchase details, including a purchase token, product identifier, purchase state, and signed purchase receipt, to verify, restore, and acknowledge full-game access. A verified receipt is stored locally so previously verified access can work offline. Payment-card details are not provided to the developer by this app. Purchase checks communicate with Google Play when available. Advertising and analytics The app contains no advertisements, advertising SDKs, developer-operated analytics, or developer-configured crash-reporting service. Google Play Billing includes Google Play services and data-transport components which may process purchase-related service and diagnostic information under Google’s policies. The app does not call location APIs or request location permission. The presence of bundled Google location libraries does not give the app permission to access your location. It does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, or camera. Google Play and your Android device may process service, purchase, diagnostic, or backup information under their own settings and policies. Android backup and retention Progress and selected image copies may be included in Android backup or device transfer, depending on your device and account settings. The app's verified-purchase receipt cache is excluded from the app's configured backup rules; restore purchases through Google Play on a new device. Local data remains until removed or the app's storage is cleared. Clearing storage or uninstalling can remove progress and custom images. Android backups may be retained separately according to your account settings. Your choices and deletion You can use the demo without purchasing and use lessons and games offline once installed. You can replace custom image selections and manage Android backup settings. To remove all local app data, use Android Settings > Apps > XenoSequence: Evolution > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This also removes local progress. Contact us to request deletion of information you sent to us by email. Google controls transaction records and refund requests made through Google Play. We use that information to respond and troubleshoot, and retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. Please avoid sending payment-card details, passwords, or sensitive personal information. Young users The app is intended for people aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us by email, contact us so we can address it.
